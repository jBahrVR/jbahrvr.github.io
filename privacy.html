Privacy Policy

jBahrVR Pipeline · effective October 29, 2026

jBahrVR Pipeline ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the jBahrVR YouTube channel to read that channel's own analytics. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for read-only access:

  • YouTube Data API (youtube.readonly): the channel's own video list, titles, descriptions and public statistics.
  • YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly): the channel's own analytics, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and subscribers gained.

The app does not read email, contacts, files or any other Google data, and it does not access other people's channels or accounts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce reports for the channel owner about his own videos (for example, which topics gain subscribers and where viewers stop watching), to help plan and edit future videos.

Storage and security

All data, including the sign-in token, is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server operated by the app, and the app has no servers of its own.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Reports are kept on the owner's computer for as long as he finds them useful, and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in YouTube's security settings; after that, the app can no longer read any data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated. Questions: contact jBahrVR through the YouTube channel's About page.
